# Changelog

What changed, in human terms, newest first. For *why*, see `docs/adr/`. For the messy
detail, see `docs/JOURNAL.md`.

Format loosely follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/). No version

Write each entry for the reader of the course, not for the person who built it.

## Unreleased

### Added: lessons 5 and 6
- **Lesson 5, My room.** Describe a room or a flat at a flatshare viewing, and find out why
  a German wardrobe is a "he".
- **Lesson 6, What I like doing.** Say what you like doing with *gern*, English's *yearn*
  in disguise, and every present-tense form of a regular verb.
- **A tale after lesson 6:** Frau Holle, the woman who makes it snow.
- **Tales are on the course page**, between the lessons they follow.
- Your dictionary now has 145 words.

### Added: lessons 3 and 4
- **Lesson 3, Spell it out.** Spell your name the German way, count to a hundred, give
  your age and phone number, and find out why German counts like a nursery rhyme.
- **Lesson 4, At the bakery.** Order rolls and a pretzel, ask what the cake costs, and
  pay, and meet der, die and das, which English had too.
- Your dictionary now has 96 words.

### Added: the first two lessons
- **Lesson 1, Hallo!** Greet anyone at any time of day, say your name and ask theirs, and
  find out why Germans ask *how* you are called.
- **Lesson 2, Where are you from?** Say where you are from, where you live and what you
  speak, and discover the sentence English and German once said word for word.
- Your dictionary now has their 46 words.

### Added: the book's structure
- A title page with a **Begin / Continue** button that remembers where you were.
- The **course map**: twelve levels from A1 to C2, every planned lesson, with a wax seal
  on the lessons you have finished and a ribbon on the one to read next.
- **Lesson pages** with a reading-progress bar and "turn the page" links; reaching the end
  of a lesson marks it finished.
- **Your dictionary** (`/words`): every word met so far, filterable by der / die / das,
  verbs and phrases.
- On phones, a tab bar at the bottom (Home, Course, Words).

### Added: the look of the book
- A design page (`/design`) shows the colours, type, ornaments and every lesson component:
  parchment and ink by day (Vellum), the same book by candlelight at night. The theme
  follows your device and can be switched with the button at the top; your choice is
  remembered on this device.
- German lines can be heard with the listen button, using your device's own German voice.
  If your device has none, the page says so.

### Added: the project's plan
- The course's design, roadmap, reader needs and first decisions are written down
  (`docs/`). Nothing to read yet; lessons 1–2 come at the end of Phase 0.
