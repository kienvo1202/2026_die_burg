Two dots change the vowel. English has no match for *ö* and *ü*: the lips do the work.

- **ü**: say "ee", then round your lips as if to whistle: <Say de="fünf" />,
  <Say de="Grüß Gott!" />
- **ö**: say "ay", then round your lips the same way: <Say de="Brötchen" />,
  <Say de="Österreich" />
**Still in the fog:** *ä* (as in <span lang="de">*Bäckerei*</span>), and how to type umlauts
without the keys, later in A1.
