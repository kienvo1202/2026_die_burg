English has one *where*. German splits it by direction.

| | means | example |
|---|---|---|
| <Say de="wo" /> | where (at) | <Say de="Wo wohnst du?" /> |
| <Say de="woher" /> | where from | <Say de="Woher kommst du?" /> |

**Remember it by:** *whence comest thou?* is <Say de="Woher kommst du?" />, word for word.

**Watch out:** "Wo kommst du?" on its own is a common mistake. You will also hear
<span lang="de">*Wo kommst du her?*</span>: that is *woher* split in two, and good German.

**Still in the fog:** *wohin* (where to), later in A1.
