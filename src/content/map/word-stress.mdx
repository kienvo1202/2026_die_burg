German stresses the **first syllable** of most simple words: <Say de="wandern" />,
<Say de="schwimmen" />, <Say de="arbeiten" />.

**Some words stress a later syllable,** often ones borrowed from French or Latin:
*Problem* (pro-BLEM), and <Say de="natürlich" /> (na-TÜR-lich), built on *Natur*.

**Regional:** *Kaffee* is KAF-feh in most of Germany, kaf-FEH in Austria.
