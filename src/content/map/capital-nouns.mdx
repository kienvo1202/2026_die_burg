Every German noun starts with a capital letter, wherever it stands in the sentence:
<span lang="de">*Brot*</span>, <span lang="de">*Kaffee*</span>,
<span lang="de">*Mädchen*</span>. It makes nouns easy to spot on a page.

Polite **Sie** has a capital too: <Say de="Wie heißen Sie?" />. Small *sie* means something else,
which you will meet later in A1.
