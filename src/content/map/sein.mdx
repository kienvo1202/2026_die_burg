*To be* is irregular in German just as *am, are, is* is in English. Learn it as it is.

| | |
|---|---|
| **ich** | <Say de="ich bin" /> |
| **du** | <Say de="du bist" /> |
| **er · sie · es** | <Say de="er ist" /> |
| **wir** | <Say de="wir sind" /> |
| **ihr** | <Say de="ihr seid" /> |
| **sie · Sie** | <Say de="sie sind" /> |

**Use it for:** your name (<Say de="Ich bin Aylin." />), your age
(<Say de="Ich bin vierunddreißig." />), and what things are like
(<Say de="Das Zimmer ist klein." />).

**Remember it by:** *bin* is a relative of English *be*, and *ist* of *is*.
