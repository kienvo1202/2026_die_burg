*To be* is irregular in German just as *am, are, is* is in English. Learn it as it is.

| | |
|---|---|
| **ich** | <Say de="ich bin" /> |
| **du** | <Say de="du bist" /> |
| **Sie** | <Say de="Sie sind" /> |

**Use it for:** your name (<Say de="Ich bin Aylin." />), and asking who someone is
(<Say de="Du bist Diego, oder?" />).

**Remember it by:** *bin* is a relative of English *be*.

**Still in the fog:** <span lang="de">*er ist, wir sind, ihr seid*</span>, later in A1.1.
