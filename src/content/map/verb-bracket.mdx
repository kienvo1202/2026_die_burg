When a verb comes in two parts, the parts **frame the sentence**. In a statement, the
first stands second and the other waits at the very end.

- <Say de="Ich kann gut kochen." />
- <Say de="Am Sonntag müssen wir früh fahren." />
- <Say de="Ich will dein Freund sein." />

English keeps the two verbs together ("I can cook"); German puts the rest of the
sentence between them.
