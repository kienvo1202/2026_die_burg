Two vowel pairs look alike and sound nothing alike. **Say the English name of the second
letter.**

| spelling | sounds like | examples |
|---|---|---|
| **ei** | "eye" | <Say de="heißen" />, <Say de="drei" />, <Say de="eins" /> |
| **ie** | "ee" | <Say de="Sie" />, <Say de="wie" />, <Say de="vier" /> |

<Say de="Auf Wiedersehen!" /> has *ie*, not *ei*: "owf VEE-der-zay-en".
