**ß** is a sharp, hissing *s*, never a B. Its name is <Say de="Eszett" />, "ess-tset".

- <Say de="heißen" />: "HY-sen"
- <Say de="dreißig" />: thirty
- <Say de="Grüß Gott!" />

Switzerland doesn't use ß at all; the Swiss write *ss*.
