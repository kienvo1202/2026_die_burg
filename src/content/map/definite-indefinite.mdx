*The* has three shapes; *a* has two.

| | *the* | *a* |
|---|---|---|
| *der* words | <Say de="der Kuchen" /> | <Say de="ein Kuchen" /> |
| *die* words | <Say de="die Brezel" /> | <Say de="eine Brezel" /> |
| *das* words | <Say de="das Stück" /> | <Say de="ein Stück" /> |

**Watch out when ordering:** for *der* words, *ein* becomes *einen*
(<span lang="de">*einen Kaffee*</span>). Until a later lesson explains it, order *die* and
*das* words, or use a number: <Say de="zwei Kaffee, bitte" />.
