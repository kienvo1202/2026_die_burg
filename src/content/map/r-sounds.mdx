At the start of a syllable, German *r* is a soft gargle at the back of the throat:
<Say de="reisen" />. In Switzerland and parts of Bavaria and Austria it is often rolled on
the tongue instead; both are correct.

At the end of a word, *r* nearly disappears. **-er** becomes a short, open "a"
(<Say de="wandern" />), and after a long vowel the *r* melts into a soft "a":
<Say de="wir" /> ("veer-a"), <Say de="hier" />.

**The English-speaker's mistake** is adding a full English *r*. Leave it out and you sound
far more German.
