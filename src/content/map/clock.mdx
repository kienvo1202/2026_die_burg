For the half hour, German counts **towards the coming hour**: <Say de="halb drei" /> is
2:30, half of the way to three. Quarters count from the hour, as in English.

| | |
|---|---|
| 2:00 | <Say de="zwei Uhr" /> |
| 2:15 | <Say de="Viertel nach zwei" /> |
| 2:30 | <Say de="halb drei" /> |
| 2:45 | <Say de="Viertel vor drei" /> |
| 2:50 | <Say de="zehn vor drei" /> |

**The trap:** British "half three" is 3:30, which is <Say de="halb vier" />.

**On timetables and for appointments:** the 24-hour clock, read as written. 14:30 is
<Say de="vierzehn Uhr dreißig" />.

**Ask:** <Say de="Wie spät ist es?" />

**Regional:** in parts of eastern and southern Germany and in Austria, 2:15 can be
<span lang="de">*viertel drei*</span> (lesson 10).

**Still in the fog:** dates (*am dritten Mai*), in A1.2.
