German has two words for *you*, and you choose every time you speak.

| <Say de="du" /> | <Say de="Sie" /> |
|---|---|
| friends, family, children | strangers, shop staff, officials |
| people your age in casual places | older people, and at work |
| fellow students, online | until someone offers *du* |

**The verb changes with it:** <Say de="Woher kommst du?" /> but
<Say de="Woher kommen Sie?" />.

**When in doubt, use *Sie*:** being too polite costs nothing, and the other person can
always offer *du*.

**Remember it by:** English made the same choice once. *Thou* was *du*; *you* was *Sie*.
English settled it by being polite to everyone.
