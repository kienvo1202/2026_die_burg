You come **from** a place with <Say de="aus" />, and live **in** it with
<Say de="in" />.

- <Say de="Ich komme aus Mexiko." en="I'm from Mexico" />
- <Say de="Ich wohne in Leipzig." en="I live in Leipzig" />

**The question decides the answer:** <Say de="Woher kommst du?" /> → *aus*;
<Say de="Wo wohnst du?" /> → *in*.

**A few countries bring their article along:** <Say de="aus der Schweiz" />. Keep it as a
phrase for now.
