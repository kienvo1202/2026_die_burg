The everyday German way to say "I like doing" isn't a verb. German does the verb and adds
<Say de="gern" />, *gladly*, after it (not "ich gern lese"):

- <Say de="Ich lese gern." />: I like reading.
- <Say de="Ich lese sehr gern." />: I love reading.
- <Say de="Ich tanze nicht gern." />: I don't like dancing.
- <Say de="Wir spielen gern Fußball." />: we like playing football.

**To ask:** <Say de="Was machst du gern?" /> · <Say de="Was macht ihr gern?" />

**Remember it by:** *gern* is a cousin of English **yearn**. Old English *georn* meant eager.

**Not "Ich mag lesen":** for activities, use *gern*.
