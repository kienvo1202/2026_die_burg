Three letters that don't sound the way English expects:

- **w** sounds like English *v*: <Say de="wie" />, <Say de="wohnen" />, <Say de="Willkommen!" />
- **s** before a vowel sounds like *z* in most of Germany: <Say de="Sie" />,
  <Say de="sieben" />. In the south, Austria and Switzerland it is often a sharper *s*.

**Still in the fog:** *v*, which usually sounds like *f* (<span lang="de">*vier*</span>), later in A1.
