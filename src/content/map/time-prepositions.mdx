Two small words for *when*:

- **am** for days: <Say de="am Montag" />, <Say de="am Samstag" />
- **um** for clock times: <Say de="um drei Uhr" />, <Say de="um halb drei" />

Together: <Say de="am Donnerstag um Viertel nach drei" />.

**The days** are all *der* words: <Say de="der Montag" />, <Say de="der Dienstag" />,
<Say de="der Mittwoch" />, <Say de="der Donnerstag" />, <Say de="der Freitag" />,
<Say de="der Samstag" />, <Say de="der Sonntag" />.

Taught in lesson 10.
