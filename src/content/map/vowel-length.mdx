German vowels come long or short, and the spelling usually tells you which.

- **A double consonant after a vowel makes it short:** <Say de="Butter" />,
  <Say de="Wasser" />, <Say de="Bett" />.
- **h after a vowel, or a doubled vowel, makes it long:** <Say de="Kühlschrank" />,
  <Say de="Tee" />, <Say de="leer" />.

Short <Say de="Milch" />, long <Say de="Käse" />: getting the length right matters a lot
for being understood.
