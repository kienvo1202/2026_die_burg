Put the question word first and the verb second. The person comes after the verb.

| question word | verb | person |
|---|---|---|
| <Say de="Wie" /> | <span lang="de">heißt</span> | <span lang="de">du?</span> |
| <Say de="Wie" /> | <span lang="de">heißen</span> | <span lang="de">Sie?</span> |

<Say de="Wie heißt du?" /> asks *how* you are called, not *what* your name is. A
word-for-word <span lang="de">*Was ist dein Name?*</span> is understood, but sounds translated.
In formal settings, on the phone or with officials, you will also hear
<span lang="de">*Wie ist Ihr Name?*</span>: that is natural, and a later lesson explains it.
