At the end of a word, **b** sounds like *p*, **d** like *t*, **g** like *k*:

- <Say de="das Bad" />: "baht"
- <Say de="und" />: "oont"
- <Say de="Guten Tag!" />: "tahk"

Add an ending and the soft sound returns: <span lang="de">*Bad*</span> ("baht"),
<span lang="de">*Bäder*</span> ("BAY-der").

**One exception:** final **-ig** sounds like *-ich* in standard German:
<Say de="günstig" />, "GÜN-stich". In the south you will also hear "-ik".
