In a statement, **the verb is the second building block**. Usually the subject comes
first; start with something else and the subject steps behind the verb.

- <Say de="Ich arbeite am Dienstag." />
- <Say de="Am Dienstag arbeite ich." />
- <Say de="Um zwölf habe ich Zeit." />

**A block, not a word:** *am Mittwoch um zehn* is one block.

**Outside the count:** *und* and *aber*: <Say de="Aber um zwölf habe ich Zeit." />

**In English:** only scraps survive, as in "Here comes the bus".

**Still in the fog:** other first places (*heute*, *dann*, a whole clause), and what
happens to a second verb, which goes to the end (lesson 12).
